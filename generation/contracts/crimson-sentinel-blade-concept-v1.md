## Locked identity

The weapon belongs visually to the accepted Crimson Sentinel design: Captain Aldric Thorne, a
reanimated Outer Watch captain whose purpose outlived the person. The character's visual thesis is
**duty becoming architecture**. The blade should therefore resemble a gate-bar, portcullis, or
sealed threshold made into a weapon—not a trophy, ceremonial jewel, demonic relic, or agile
duelist's sword.

Preserve these source-derived facts:

- monumental two-handed greatsword scale, massive but modelable for a game asset
- straight, broad, double-edged blackened-steel blade with a long diamond point
- compact, heavy angular guard with downward portcullis-like shoulders
- dark wrapped grip and squat faceted iron pommel
- extensive pitting, edge nicks, corrosion, and permanent dark-crimson staining
- restrained blood energy visible inside recessed blade channels and around the grip/gauntlet
  junction; no open flame, external aura, or neon wash
- current visual baseline of the sword fused to the Sentinel's right gauntlet by blood magic,
  with crystallized welds and a few broken chain remnants

## Speculative physical design for this study

The dominant side elevation should resolve the accepted promotional silhouette into a production-
useful object:

- a thick central spine runs from the guard to the final third of the blade, giving the weapon the
  load-bearing mass of a door brace
- twin recessed fullers branch into restrained arterial channels rather than decorative runes;
  their contained crimson glow should feel pressurized under iron
- the cutting edges remain mostly straight and functional, with irregular century-worn nicks but
  no saw teeth
- the guard is short and architectural: two heavy downward quillons like broken gate brackets,
  with no skulls, horns, wings, or crown
- the grip is long enough for two hands but the lower half is partly consumed by blackened
  blood-crystal growth, suggesting the weapon can no longer be released normally
- the right gauntlet close-up shows armor plates and fingers fused around the upper grip by
  deliberate crystallized-blood seams; this is a magical weld, not gore and not a severed limb
- several broken chain links emerge from the fusion collar, echoing the Sentinel's willing prison
- the point is reinforced for resting on stone between patrol cycles, with abrasion and dark
  mineral deposits at the final inches

These construction details are visual proposals only. Do not invent an official weapon name,
inscription, maker, ability, soul, voice, faction rank, or historical event.

## Composition

Create a clean 3:2 landscape weapon-concept sheet on a warm gray, lightly worn neutral field that
belongs to the accepted Crimson Sentinel promotional family without copying its poster design.

- dominant full side elevation spans most of the sheet from lower-left to upper-right, with the
  complete pommel and point visible and generous safe margins
- one smaller three-quarter view shows blade thickness, central spine, and guard depth
- one tight construction inset shows the gauntlet-to-grip blood-crystal fusion and broken chain
  collar; show only the armored gauntlet and a short forearm cuff, never the full character
- one small material inset shows blackened iron, recessed arterial channel, worn edge, and
  contained crimson light
- use unlabeled inset framing or subtle tonal separation only; no callout text, arrows, numbers,
  scale labels, fake handwriting, or UI chrome

The sheet should prioritize an immediately readable silhouette and modelable construction over
cinematic atmosphere. Soft frontal studio lighting should reveal thickness, wear, edge condition,
and material transitions. The weapon is solemn, industrial, ritualized, and tragic—not ornate for
ornament's sake.
