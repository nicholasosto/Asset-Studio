## Locked canon

- This is the Great Sundering of the second reincarnation, not the Exoverse reset that ended the
  first reincarnation.
- The event occurs in one catastrophic moment, centuries before the present.
- The Soul Core is the crystallized nexus, source, anchor, and regulator of soul energy.
- The Core shatters into exactly five major fragments. One unified Creation Plane becomes five
  fragment-attuned realities, territories, or civilizations.
- The release kills millions, reshapes continents, divides universal soul energy into five
  attunements, and weakens the boundary between Creation and the overlapping spectral layer.
- The event's cause remains unresolved. Do not show an attacker, weapon, responsible faction,
  Exoverse figure, intentional control mechanism, or privileged theory.
- Do not identify the fifth fragment as the Void Domain or encode a Void-domain emblem. Its
  identity remains visually unpinned.
- The Fateless arise later in the borderlands and hold no fragment of their own. They are not the
  cause of the Sundering.

## Shared visual language

High-end cinematic game-environment concept art in stylized realism, combining Soul Steel's
cyber-gothic register with restrained cosmic horror. Religious architecture and industrial
infrastructure should feel like one civilization rather than separate fantasy and science-fiction
layers: ribbed metal vaults, monumental pylons, oxidized ceremonial machinery, luminous conduits,
and cathedral-scale negative space. Use painterly atmosphere with crisp focal geometry, believable
scale, rich but controlled material cues, and readable silhouettes.

The Soul Core is an immense faceted crystalline nexus rather than a literal anatomical heart. Its
internal light suggests layered consciousness: bone-white at the center, prismatic spectral
strata, and thin metallic inclusions like a cosmic reliquary. The five fragment frequencies may
separate into restrained crimson, fungal green-violet, pale spirit blue-white, machine
amber-cobalt, and one unnamed opalescent/ashen frequency. The fifth must not use a recognizable
domain sigil or obvious black-hole/Void iconography.

The three images should feel like one visual family without pretending to be consecutive camera
frames. Use a 3:2 landscape composition, one dominant focal event, strong depth layers, a tiny
human-scale silhouette language only where needed to communicate enormity, and enough calm space
around the Core for the fracture geometry to remain legible.

## Variant `before` — the regulator

Depict the final quiet instant before the Great Sundering. The intact Soul Core hangs above the
unified Creation Plane at the nexus of an immense cyber-gothic world-city. Luminous conduits carry
one continuous soul-energy frequency through mountains, towers, bridges, and ceremonial
machinery. The Core is serene but under impossible pressure: five hairline internal fault planes
are barely visible inside the crystal, not yet broken and not caused by any visible external
force. The surrounding world still reads as one connected geography.

Composition: low, distant establishing view across the unified city toward the elevated Core;
Core above center; architecture frames rather than eclipses it; foreground silhouettes remain
small. Lighting is pre-storm bone-white and muted metal, with a restrained prismatic tension inside
the Core. Mood: sacred stillness, the breath before a verdict.

## Variant `shatter` — one world made five

Depict the exact single moment of the Great Sundering. The Soul Core ruptures from within into
exactly five enormous crystalline fragments, each following a distinct radial trajectory. A
pressure wave crosses the Creation Plane; continents and monumental structures shear along five
broad resonance lines; universal soul-light separates into five controlled frequencies. The
spectral layer becomes visible through a vast translucent tear, like a second misregistered world
seen through cracked cathedral glass.

Composition: wide elevated three-quarter view with the Core at the upper-center focal point and
the five fragment trajectories clearly countable. The landscape below supplies scale, consequence,
and directional fracture lines. The moment is violent but not gory. Lighting is a bone-white
central breach with restrained five-frequency color separation, deep charcoal occlusion, and
metallic atmospheric debris. Mood: instantaneous cosmic trauma, enormous silence rendered as
pressure.

## Variant `aftermath` — the wound remains

Depict the immediate stillness after the blast, before centuries of domain civilization develop.
Five distant fragment-falls burn at separated horizons across a newly fractured Creation Plane.
The old unified city and landscape are broken, partially displaced, and crossed by regions of
different atmospheric frequency. The spectral echo of the pre-Sundering world remains offset
behind the ruins, visible through the torn boundary. Soul-light drains through cracked conduits;
small surviving silhouettes stand in the borderland seam without being identifiable characters.

Composition: ground-level panoramic view from a broken civic or ceremonial threshold; one ruined
arch frames the fractured horizon; five distinct fragment-falls are readable but geographically
distant; no single fall is privileged as the "true" fragment. Lighting is ash-dim with lingering
bone-white fracture light, restrained domain-frequency glows, and long cold shadows. Mood:
survival inside a cosmological wound, not victory or closure.
