## Source lock

The established Soul Steel alignment set is Blood, Decay, Spirit, Robot, and Fateless. Void is a
brain-native speculative candidate and must be visibly marked as such.

- **Blood** treats life force as raw material: drain, corrupt, empower, regenerate. Its mark
  language is thorned circulation, a controlled droplet or seed, a closed metabolic loop, and
  dark crimson organic pressure.
- **Decay** embodies patient entropy: corrosion, attrition, spreading debilitation, and the
  certainty that constructed things return to nothing. Its mark language is an eroding ring,
  incomplete spiral, spore-like pitting, crumbling edges, and cold oxidized green.
- **Spirit** is attunement between physical and metaphysical states: resonance, redirection,
  wards, purification, and pale threads connecting living things. Its mark language is
  concentric resonance, linked nodes, a frost-glass ward, and two states in harmonic alignment.
- **Robot** treats soul energy as a reliable fuel to build, refine, and perfect. Its mark language
  is a calibrated aperture, modular circuit paths, a measured battery core, and industrial
  geometry in brushed steel with restrained electric-blue or furnace-amber energy.
- **Fateless** rejects a single binding domain: no chains, no crowns, only will. Its mark language
  is an open or broken bond, five unresolved shards or paths, a fractured compass, and multiple
  restrained frequencies that converge without locking.
- **Void — speculative** is negation and denial: absorption, silence, nullification, and removal
  of options. Its mark language is a missing center, consumed geometry, eclipsed ring, and a
  deliberate negative-space cut. It must read as a candidate outside the established five.

## Shared sigil grammar

Design a coherent Soul Steel icon system, not six unrelated logos. Every panel contains one large
core sigil and four smaller variations derived from the same domain idea. Across all six panels:

- use a forged circular or reliquary frame, a memorable central cutout, three to five structural
  strokes, and a bold silhouette readable at small UI size
- vary construction meaningfully: minimal glyph, sealed crest, combat badge, relic stamp, and
  compact UI mark, without labeling those variants
- keep the icons graphic and object-like, with restrained dimensional embossing; no landscapes,
  characters, weapons, or full narrative scenes inside the marks
- make differences legible by silhouette before color while retaining a unified cyber-gothic,
  ancient-industrial visual language
- preserve clear negative space and avoid dense filigree that collapses at thumbnail scale

## Domain-specific visual lock

- Blood: deep crimson, blackened iron, thorn tips, circulating inner channel; not merely a generic
  blood droplet.
- Decay: cold moss-green, oxidized bronze, ash-gray corrosion, one visibly eroding side; no
  biohazard or radiation-symbol resemblance.
- Spirit: pale cyan-white, silver, faint violet, paired resonance rings and linked light threads;
  no angel wings, church cross, or generic halo.
- Robot: brushed steel, dark graphite, precise blue core with a tiny amber furnace accent,
  measured modular geometry; not merely a generic gear.
- Fateless: dark neutral metal with five restrained color frequencies, a broken/open bond, no
  center crown, and no single dominant domain color.
- Void — speculative: near-black center represented by true negative space, ultraviolet and
  ice-white rim, geometry visibly removed rather than added. Give this panel a subtle dashed
  border or small stamped distinction so it cannot be mistaken for an established alignment.
