## Established source lock

Kael is a weathered knight who survived the Age of Sundering beneath the roots of the World-Ash.
His forearms have bark-hardened skin and thorn-like scars, and his left eye carries persistent
green luminescence. He was born to Greymarch timber-cutters, conscripted into the Ashen Order, and
deserted when its commanders ordered a retreat that would abandon civilians. He now wanders
without faction, guarding Veil-thin fractures. He is quiet, observant, dryly humorous, stubbornly
protective, Fateless-aligned, stone-aspected, and burdened by survival.

The Ashen Order supplies only two established clothing signals: grey cloaks and burnished steel.
Its original creed was “We are the last ember.” No Kael weapon, helmet, insignia, facial design,
body proportions, or World-Ash visual design is established.
