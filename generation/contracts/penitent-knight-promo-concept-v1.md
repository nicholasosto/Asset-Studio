## Locked canon and physical design

The Penitent Knight is a decorated Crimson Order officer whose martial discipline and
uncompromising personal code have become the Order's slow break. The Knight believes sacrifice
should be willing, not extracted. Show one tall, heavy-shouldered, restrained figure whose threat
comes from control rather than rage.

Preserve the established full-body design:

- fully enclosed cracked-obsidian helm with a narrow crimson eye slit and restrained crown-like
  points from the reference, never a visible face
- broad gothic pauldrons, gorget, layered breastplate, heavy gauntlets, plate skirt, greaves, and
  disciplined upright proportions
- ancient obsidian-forged mythic plate over a dark gunmetal understructure
- worn dark-crimson battle-cloth and cloak pieces that support the silhouette without hiding the
  armor construction
- etched personal-honor markings and old Blood Domain ritual sigils
- advanced technology visible only as faint red micro-circuitry beneath cracks, sensor glints
  inside the helm, cable-like ceremonial filigree, and a subdued reactor glow beneath the
  breastplate; sacred and ancient first, engineered second
- the Vowbrand held at rest in the right hand, blade point grounded beside the boot, with its
  circular oath-seal guard and thin red channels clearly readable

The signature visual beat is **the vow kept after the Order forgot it**. The Knight stands
perfectly upright, but the head and Vowbrand angle slightly away from the official Crimson Order
seal motif, creating a restrained visual refusal without turning the pose into open rebellion.

finish, slightly exaggerated heroic proportions, crisp silhouette edges, convincing cracked
